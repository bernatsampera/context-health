# Direction

IndoEuroMap shows how multicultural each European city and neighbourhood is, with our own data.

## Who it is for

- People who want to know how multicultural their city or district is, and how it compares.
- Journalists, researchers, and city staff who need neighbourhood numbers across countries.

## What we promise

- One method for all of Europe. One source of truth: IndoEuroMap data.
- Numbers for each neighbourhood, city, region, and country.
- Clear limits on one methodology page.

## What we do not do in this iteration

- No new SEO framework. No URL change. No title or H1 change before the 3 November reading.
- No method research. One classifier fix only. It gives data version 2.
- No money work. The report page and the research page stay as they are.
- No Instagram. No browser automation on any social account.
- No new tool layer. No knowledge tree, no context system, no session journal.
- No tests. A real run proves each change.
